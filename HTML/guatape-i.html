GUATAPE

Guatape is one of the most recognized tourist destinations in Antioquia and Colombia. It is famous for its colorful streets, its decorative baseboards, its natural landscapes and the enormous Rock of Guatape.

The municipality is surrounded by a large reservoir, creating beautiful landscapes ideal for enjoying water activities and nature.

Its streets, decorated houses and plazas make exploring Guatape an experience full of color, culture and tradition.

HISTORY AND TRADITION

The town of the decorative baseboards

Guatape preserves one of the most characteristic architectural traditions in Colombia: the decorative baseboards that adorn the facades of many of its houses.

These designs represent different elements of everyday life, animals, plants, symbols and traditions of its inhabitants.

Thanks to its colors and designs, the streets of Guatape have become one of the most recognizable urban landscapes in the country.
