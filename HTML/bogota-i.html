BOGOTA

Bogota is the capital of Colombia and one of the most important cities in the country. It is located in the Andes Mountains, on an extensive plateau.

The city combines history, culture, gastronomy, art and architecture with a modern and diverse urban life.

Its different neighborhoods and cultural spaces allow visitors to discover different sides of Colombia in a single destination.

HISTORY AND CULTURE

A city full of history

Bogota has a history that dates back to the pre-Hispanic era and was later transformed during the colonial period.

The historic center preserves buildings, squares and streets that allow visitors to discover part of the city's past.

Today, Bogota is also an important cultural center, with museums, theaters, libraries and different spaces dedicated to art.
