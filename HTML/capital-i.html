CAPITAL OF COLOMBIA

Bogota is the capital of Colombia and is located in the Andean region, on an extensive plateau surrounded by mountains.

It is the main political, administrative, economic, educational and cultural center of the country.

The city combines its important historical heritage with modern urban spaces, universities, museums, parks and great cultural diversity.

Thanks to its national importance, Bogota is one of the main meeting points in Colombia and receives visitors from different regions and countries.

HISTORY OF BOGOTA

The history of Bogota is the result of centuries of transformations. Before becoming the capital of Colombia, this territory was home to important indigenous communities and later changed during the colonial period and the independence process.

01

THE MUISCAS

Before the arrival of the Spanish, the territory where Bogota is currently located was mainly inhabited by the Muisca people.

This community developed an organized society and had an important relationship with agriculture, trade and different cultural traditions.

02

ARRIVAL OF THE SPANISH

During the 16th century, the Spanish arrived in the territory and began a process of conquest and establishment of new political and social structures.

The territory gained new administrative importance within the region.

03

COLONIAL PERIOD

During the colonial period, Bogota became an important political, religious and administrative center of the territory.

The architecture and some areas of the historic center preserve part of the influence of this period.

04

INDEPENDENCE

Bogota played a fundamental role in the independence process of Colombia.

On July 20, 1810, one of the most important events of this process took place, beginning a new stage in the history of the country.

05

GROWTH OF THE CITY

During the 19th and 20th centuries, Bogota began to grow and expand significantly.

The arrival of new institutions, transportation systems, industries and educational centers contributed to the development of the city.

06

MODERN BOGOTA

Today, Bogota is a modern, diverse and dynamic city that combines its historical heritage with new urban areas.

As the capital of Colombia, it concentrates important political, economic, educational and cultural institutions.

BOGOTA TODAY

Bogota is currently one of the most important cities in Colombia. Its growth has allowed the development of different sectors and has made the city an important center for education, business, culture and tourism.

The capital also stands out for its diversity. People from different regions of the country live together in a city that combines Colombian traditions with new forms of cultural expression.

Its historical heritage, cultural spaces and urban activity make Bogota a city that represents an important part of Colombian identity.
