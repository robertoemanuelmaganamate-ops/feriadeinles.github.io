ABOUT THE VUELTIAO HAT

A tradition that never goes out of style

The Vueltiao Hat is one of the most representative handicrafts of Colombia. It is handmade from caña flecha and represents the culture of the Caribbean region.

🌿

caña flecha.

Handmade Craftsmanship

Unique Design

Its shapes and colors reflect the identity and creativity of Colombian culture.

⭐

National Symbol

It is internationally recognized as one of Colombia's most important handicraft symbols.

WEAVING QUALITY

Different levels of weaving

The quality of the hat can vary depending on the number of turns and the fineness of its weave.

Traditional

Simple and durable weaving for everyday use.
