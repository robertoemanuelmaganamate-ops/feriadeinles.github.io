NATIONAL TEAM

The Colombia National Team represents the country in major international football competitions.

The team is recognized by its yellow, blue and red colors, the same colors that represent the national flag.

Football is one of the great passions of Colombians, and the national team has become an important symbol of national identity and pride.

HISTORY

A passion that unites the country

The Colombia National Team has built an important history in South American and international football.

Throughout the years, different generations of footballers have represented the country and created important moments for the fans.

The team has participated in different editions of the Copa America and the FIFA World Cup, becoming one of the most recognized teams in South America.
