COLOMBIAN POPULATION

Colombia is a country with a diverse population distributed among its different natural regions.

Its inhabitants have different customs, traditions, ways of speaking and cultural expressions that are part of Colombian identity.

Most of the population is concentrated in cities and urban areas, although there are also numerous rural communities.

This population diversity is the result of different historical processes and the mixing of cultures that has occurred over time.

A DIVERSE POPULATION

The diversity of the Colombian population is one of the country's most important characteristics. Differences between regions are reflected in music, gastronomy, festivals, customs and ways of life.

This cultural variety gives each region of Colombia its own identity, while all of them are part of the same national identity.

The Colombian population continues to change with the growth of cities, internal population movements and new generations.
