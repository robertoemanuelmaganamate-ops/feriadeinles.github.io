DISCOVER COCORA VALLEY

A unique landscape in Colombia

Cocora Valley is one of the most representative natural landscapes in Colombia. It is located in the department of Quindio and is part of the Coffee Cultural Landscape.

It is famous for its enormous wax palms, considered one of Colombia's great natural symbols. Its green mountains and extensive landscapes make this place a perfect destination for enjoying nature.

LOCATION

Salento, Quindio

NATURE

Forests and wax palms

LANDSCAPE

Mountains and valleys

ACTIVITIES

Hiking and exploration

WHAT CAN YOU DO?

Hiking

Explore the trails and enjoy the natural landscapes.

Photography

Capture the mountains, forests and famous wax palms.

Wildlife Watching

Discover the flora and fauna that inhabit this wonderful place.

WHY VISIT IT?

Cocora Valley combines nature, adventure and breathtaking landscapes. It is an ideal place to discover one of Colombia's most important symbols and enjoy an experience surrounded by mountains and vegetation.
