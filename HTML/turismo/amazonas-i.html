Colombian Amazon

Adventure in the heart of the rainforest

DISCOVER THE COLOMBIAN AMAZON

An adventure in the rainforest

The Colombian Amazon is one of the most important natural regions in the country. Its vast tropical rainforest is home to a great variety of plants, animals and ecosystems.

Leticia is the main city in this region and serves as a starting point to explore the rainforest, rivers and communities that live in this impressive territory.

LOCATION

Leticia, Amazonas

RAINFOREST

Tropical rainforest

BIODIVERSITY

Great variety of species

RIVERS

Amazon River and its tributaries

WHAT CAN YOU DO?

Explore the rainforest

Discover trails and experience the incredible Amazonian nature.

Explore the river

Sail along the rivers and admire the landscapes of the region.

Observe wildlife

Discover some of the species that live in the rainforest.

WHY VISIT IT?

The Colombian Amazon offers a unique experience for those who want to connect with nature. Its landscapes, rivers and biodiversity make this region one of the most special places to discover the natural richness of Colombia.
