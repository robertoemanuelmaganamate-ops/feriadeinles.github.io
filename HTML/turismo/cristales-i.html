DISCOVER CAÑO CRISTALES

The most colorful river in Colombia

Caño Cristales is one of the most amazing natural destinations in Colombia. It is known as the river of seven colors because of the different shades that appear in its waters.

It is located in the Sierra de la Macarena, in the department of Meta. Its waters, rock formations and natural landscapes create a unique setting that attracts visitors from all over the world.

LOCATION

RIVER

WHAT CAN YOU DO?

Exploration

WHY VISIT IT?

Caño Cristales offers a unique experience thanks to its colors, crystal-clear waters and beautiful landscapes. It is a perfect destination for those who want to discover one of the most special natural wonders of Colombia.
