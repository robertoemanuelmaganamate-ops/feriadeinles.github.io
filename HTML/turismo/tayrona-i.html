Tayrona Park

Nature, beaches and adventure in one place

DISCOVER TAYRONA PARK

Where the jungle meets the sea

Tayrona National Natural Park is one of the most recognized natural destinations in Colombia. It is located on the Caribbean coast, near Santa Marta, and combines beaches, mountains and tropical rainforest.

Its landscapes allow visitors to enjoy nature while walking along trails surrounded by vegetation and discovering some of the most beautiful beaches of the Colombian Caribbean.

LOCATION

Santa Marta, Magdalena

BEACHES

Beaches of the Colombian Caribbean

NATURE

ADVENTURE

WHY VISIT IT?

Tayrona Park is an ideal destination for those looking to combine adventure, nature and relaxation. Its beaches, mountains and tropical rainforest create a unique landscape that represents the natural richness of the Colombian Caribbean.
